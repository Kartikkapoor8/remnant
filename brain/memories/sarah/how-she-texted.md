# How Sarah texted

## Compiled Truth

Lowercase, no periods, bursts of two or three short messages. "haha" not
"lol". "ya" for yes. "okok" to agree. "omw" when leaving. Stretched letters
when she felt something: "soooo", "heyyy", "nooo". Dropped apostrophes:
"dont", "im", "cant". Opened conversations with "hi hi", often "hi hi bub".
Rare emoji: 🌻 and ❤️. Said "love you" plainly and often, usually as the last
message before driving or sleeping.
