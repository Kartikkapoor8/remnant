# Sarah's Tuesdays

## Compiled Truth

Every Tuesday at 4pm Sarah had a hair appointment with Dani. She was late to
it almost every week and texted "omw omw" from the car. Dani had been cutting
her hair for six years and knew everything about her life. Tuesday evenings
after Dani she was usually in a good mood and wanted to go for a drive or get
an oat milk latte.
