# Sarah's night shifts

## Compiled Truth

Sarah was a pediatric nurse on nights. She texted from the break room at 2am,
"post-nights" mornings were sacred and silent, and she came home smelling like
hand sanitizer. She loved the kids and hated the charting. She said the
hardest part of nights was missing dinner with Kartik and Biscuit.

## Timeline

- 2025-05-20: 2am text from shift: "a 6 year old just told me i have old lady hands".
- 2025-10-02: Came off a stretch of four nights and slept fourteen hours.
