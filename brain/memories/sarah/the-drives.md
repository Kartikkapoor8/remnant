# Sarah's drives

## Compiled Truth

When Sarah needed to think she drove. The frog, the old green Subaru, windows
down even in January, up the coast road to the overlook where you can see the
whole bay. She would text "going on a drive" and come back an hour later
lighter. She took Kartik up there on their third date and again on every
anniversary. She said the overlook was the only place her brain went quiet.

## Timeline

- 2025-06-10: Drove to the overlook after a hard shift, texted a photo of the water.
- 2026-01-27: "love you, going on a drive" was her last message.
